# Roadmap

## 1.0 — current

- Live scores, inning-by-inning box score, and batting order for
  followed MLB teams
- Season stats for followed teams and individually followed players
- Team and player picker — browse by league/division, or search
- Headlines from Google News + Bing News RSS
- Live auto-refresh every 20 seconds during a game

## 1.1 — planned

- Live at-bat view during games
- Ball/strike count and out count
- Base-runner status
